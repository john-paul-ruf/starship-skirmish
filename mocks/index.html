Starship Skirmish

A 3D turn-based tactical combat game. Design point-costed warships in a deep shipyard, then field them against AI fleets in simultaneous-turn battles where momentum, collision, and exploding wreckage are as lethal as the guns.

1The ShipyardA lab, not a menu

A spreadsheet that respects you. Every choice is a trade — a high-capacity shield eats the points you wanted for a second gun; a hot engine buys initiative but leaves you thin when a lance connects. Dense rows, tabular figures, running totals, delta indicators. The design layer is first-class content.

2The SkirmishA tac display, not a space sim

Neon abstract geometry on black. Plans are made blind and resolve simultaneously. The arena is the clock — a lethal boundary, accumulating debris, armed spent missiles. Abstraction is the art direction, not placeholder art.

v1DESIGN PHASE
User Flows
Flow 1 — Build a ship (primary)
Encyclopedia→ Pick chassis→ Fit components→ Read the deltas→ Save

The loop that matters is swap → read delta → swap again. It must feel like editing a spreadsheet cell.

Flow 2 — Fight (the payoff)
Budget & draft→ Opponents→ Plot arcs blind→ Movement resolves→ Assign fire blind→ Fire resolves↶ Last fleet standing
Flow 3 — Share a build (the meta)
Copy share link→ Paste in chat→ Import preview→ Resolve collision→ Added

≤1900 characters, encoded against the versioned catalog. Nothing is written until you confirm.

Flow 5 — Return after a year (no loss, ever)
Open the app→ Migration chain runs→ ⚠ needs-refit flagged→ Re-fit or keep

The flag is a receipt, not a lock. Every build ever saved, shared, or exported must load. Forever.

Never Color Alone
Identity LegendLoad-bearing for FR-13 & NFR-Accessibility

Fleet ownership, hazard type, and missile state are each carried by color + glyph + text label, always, everywhere — roster, viewport, legend, combat log, post-match summary. A colorblind player loses zero information. This is not a courtesy; it is why the glyph set exists.

What These Mocks Fake
⚠ The 3D is faked

CSS perspective, absolute positioning, and inline SVG. The mocks prove layout, legibility, and information hierarchy at fleet scale — not that a renderer can hit 60fps with 300 hazard bodies. That is Architect's call and Gate 1's job.

⚠ Silhouettes are placeholders

FR-13 requires ship class to be distinguishable by silhouette at fleet zoom. The mocks assert it. Real geometry must re-verify it in the polish phase.

⚠ CDN Tailwind + Google Fonts

Mock-only conveniences. Both violate the offline-after-first-load requirement and neither should survive into src/. The token layer in console.css is the part meant to carry forward.

⚠ No number here is balanced

Point totals are internally consistent fixtures, not tuned values. Balance comes from the headless harness (FR-33) — with no server and no telemetry, it is the only instrument that exists.
